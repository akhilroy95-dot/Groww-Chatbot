## How do I generate my Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS?
To get your CAS, follow these steps:
You can click on the 'CAMS' button below to go directly to the CAS - CAMS+KFintech page.
CAMS

## Steps
1. Visit the official CAMS website.
2. Click on 'MF Investor.'
3. Select 'Statement' and then choose 'CAS - CAMS+KFintech.'
4. Statement Type: Detailed
5. Period: Specific Period (From 1st Jan 1990 to the present date)
6. Folio Listing: With zero balance folios
7. Email ID: Linked to your investments
8. Password: PAN number in uppercase
9. Submit the details.
10. You'll receive your CAS via email from in PDF format.
